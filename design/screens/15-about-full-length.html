This app uses virtual money for educational purposes. It is not financial, investment, or gambling advice. Betting involves risk and many bettors lose money.

How advice is made

1

The model estimates the probability of home, draw and away for each upcoming match from team strength, form, home advantage, rest days and player availability.

2

It compares that with the bookmaker's odds after removing the margin. A match is a candidate only if model probability × odds − 1 is at least 4%.

3

Stakes are a quarter of the Kelly fraction, capped at 5% of your bankroll per bet and 15% per day. Every other match is skipped, with the reason shown.
